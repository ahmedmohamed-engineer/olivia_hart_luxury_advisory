## Cinematic Principles Applied

- **Strongest moments get the most time**: Properties (12s) and Hero (10s) receive the lion's share of screen time — they are the visual and emotional anchors.
- **Minimal scrolling**: The video advances deliberately, not frantically. Each scene holds for a comfortable duration.
- **Hover states as performance**: Interactive elements (img-zoom, card shadows, overlay reveals) are showcased as demonstration moments, not rushed past.
- **Tonal drama**: The dark testimonial section creates a deliberate emotional shift — light to dark to light — like a film edit.
- **No robotic cursor**: Mouse movements are described as "drifts" and "pauses," not precise targeting. The cursor is a guide, not a performer.
- **Female luxury client focus**: The storyboard emphasizes lifestyle language ("sanctuary," "how you wish to live"), female testimonials, and the personal brand over transactional property data.
- **Professional confidence**: Every scene answers "Why should I trust this person?" — through metrics, testimonials, expertise, and design quality.

## Production Notes

- **Recording method**: Playwright browser automation or screen capture at 1080p or higher
- **Frame rate**: 30fps minimum, 60fps preferred for smooth scroll demonstration
- **Post-production**: Color grade to warm tones matching the brand palette; add subtle film grain; overlay soft piano music
- **Voiceover (optional)**: Warm, confident female narration — "This is what a personal brand looks like when it's built with intention."
- **Cursor**: Hide cursor during static holds; show during hover demonstrations; use a subtle, not oversized, cursor icon
- **Target emotion for viewer**: "I want a website like this — for myself."
