## Brand & Style

This design system embodies high-end architectural editorialism tailored for the pinnacle of luxury residential advisory across Cairo’s elite enclaves—from the modern estates of New Cairo and Katameya to Sheikh Zayed, Madinaty, and coastal private retreats in the North Coast (Ras El Hekma). 

The emotional signature is quiet luxury, architectural gravitas, and discreet exclusivity. Rejecting aggressive, high-pressure sales aesthetics, the visual language mirrors elite European and Mediterranean architectural journals (e.g., *Cereal*, *Ark Journal*, *AD Italia*). It employs vast breathing room, disciplined asymmetric image collages, generous tracking on structural micro-headers, and typographic contrast between graceful display serifs and pristine neutral sans-serifs. The interface feels collectible, grounded, timeless, and impeccably curated.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in Mediterranean stone, Nile silt clay, and warm desert alabaster. Surfaces deliberately avoid stark pure white in favor of warm, tactile ivory and linen tones.

- **Primary (`#1A1816`):** Deep espresso-charcoal. Used for dominant editorial display headlines, dark-mode breakout testimonial panels, and high-impact structural anchors.
- **Secondary (`#8C7B68`):** Weathered desert taupe. Functions as the primary accent for secondary badges, hover highlights, subtle links, and focal metadata.
- **Tertiary (`#C5B49E`):** Warm limestone sand. Used for interactive hover states, fine bounding highlights, tag containers, and refined decorative accents.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#FAF8F5`):** Soft warm ivory paper canvas. Supported by secondary surface layers in warm cream (`#F5F2EC`) and soft sand card fills (`#EFECE4`).
- **Divider & Border Tone (`#E4DDD3`):** Precision 1px hairline border tone designed to mimic architectural drafting lines.
- **Text Hierarchies:** Deep espresso (`#1A1816`) for primary titles, softened charcoal (`#2D2A26`) for body long-form, and warm mineral gray (`#4A4641` to `#706A62`) for captions and secondary notes.

## Typography

Typographic character relies on tension between Playfair Display's organic, high-contrast serif cadence and Plus Jakarta Sans’s geometric clarity. 

- **Display & Headlines:** Headlines must employ rhythmic editorial emphasis through italicized key phrases (e.g., *"BUY the Right Home & SELL with Confidence"* or *"FEEDBACK from CLIENTS"*), pairing regular serif roman letterforms with graceful italic styling. Large display serifs are set tight in tracking with generous vertical breathing room.
- **Micro-Headers & Metadata:** All category titles, geographic tags (e.g., *NEW CAIRO*, *SHEIKH ZAYED*), and step indicators must be rendered in `label-uppercase` using all-caps with generous character tracking (`0.2em` to `0.25em`).
- **Body Copy:** Long-form property reviews and bio sections use lightweight, open sans-serif typography set with ample line height (`1.7`+) to sustain an airy, printed publication atmosphere.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is governed by an asymmetric 12-column architectural grid with generous section breathing room. Layouts draw inspiration from editorial lookbooks rather than traditional SaaS or portal card grids.

- **Asymmetry & Overlap:** Feature portraits and property imagery intentionally offset along column alignments. Primary focal imagery may span 7 columns while narrative text occupies 4 columns with 1 offset column of negative space. Secondary photography vignettes overlap grid boundaries slightly or sit staggered beneath text locks.
- **Density & Cadence:** Generous vertical intervals (`space-xxl`) separate narrative movements. Section boundaries are delineated either through ample negative whitespace or single-pixel hairline horizontal dividers (`#E4DDD3`), rather than heavy background boxes.
- **Form-Factor Adaptations:**
  - **Desktop (1200px+):** 12 columns, 64px section margins, 32px gutters. Asymmetric multi-layer photo positioning.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8 columns, 32px margins, 24px gutters. Offset visuals collapse into structured staggered alignments.
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, 20px margins, 16px gutters. Stacked single-column hierarchy; display sizes reduce to preserve single-line impact for serif titles.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy rejects artificial dropshadows and heavy blurs. Depth is achieved purely through tactile surface shifts, architectural tonal layers, and hairline framing.

- **Low-Contrast Hairlines:** Modals, navigation headers, curated property panels, and section separators utilize razor-thin 1px borders in `#E4DDD3`. 
- **Tonal Layering:** Interactive surfaces shift from base canvas (`#FAF8F5`) to raised container surfaces (`#F5F2EC` or `#EFECE4`) on hover or active states.
- **Inverted Dark Anchor Sections:** Testimonial reels and footer blocks shift dramatically into deep espresso surfaces (`#1A1816` or `#131211`), establishing editorial drama, gravity, and high visual contrast at the bottom of the journey.
- **Ambient Focus:** When an image card rises on hover, elevation is conveyed via a barely perceptible ambient aura: `0 20px 40px -15px rgba(26, 24, 22, 0.06)`.

## Shapes

The geometric framework is strictly architectural, sharp, and disciplined (`roundedness: 0`). 

- **Corners:** Buttons, image frames, input containers, and badges feature crisp 90-degree corners (0px border-radius). This sharp aesthetic evokes fine stationary, museum placards, and bespoke marble fabrication.
- **Avatar & Portrait Treatment:** Circular geometry is reserved strictly for advisor portraits and agent identification badges to provide an organic, human counterpoint to the sharp structural frame.
- **Image Ratios:** Strictly governed by architectural aspect ratios: 4:5 for portraits, 3:2 for landscape interiors, and 16:9 for sweeping panoramic estates.

## Components

### Buttons & Calls to Action
- **Primary Editorial Button:** Deep espresso background (`#1A1816`), crisp white text (`#FAF8F5`), 0px border radius, tracked uppercase typography (`label-uppercase`), padded with `0.875rem 2rem`. On hover: shifts smoothly to secondary taupe (`#8C7B68`).
- **Secondary Frame Button:** Transparent background, 1px perimeter border in `#1A1816` or `#8C7B68`, matching text color. On hover: fills completely with primary espresso or subtle cream tint (`#F5F2EC`).
- **Inline Text Link:** Understated typography with a continuous 1px bottom border spaced 4px beneath the baseline; on hover, the border transitions color from `#C5B49E` to `#1A1816`.

### Cards & Property Displays
- **Lookbook Property Card:** Frameless image container with an initial 4:5 or 3:2 aspect ratio, zero border radius, followed below by an uppercase geographic micro-label (`NEW CAIRO / PALM HILLS`), serif property title, and hairline-separated specification row (Beds / Baths / Built-up Area). No heavy card containers; cards blend seamlessly into the neutral canvas.
- **Featured Collection Card:** Subtly tinted background (`#F5F2EC`), 1px hairline border (`#E4DDD3`), housing curated boutique developments.

### Chips & Metadata Tags
- Minimalist rectangular tags with 0px border radius. Finished in a subtle `#EFECE4` fill with `#4A4641` text, or transparent with a 1px `#E4DDD3` outline. Set strictly in tracked `label-uppercase` (font size 10px-11px).

### Input Fields & Inquiry Form
- Minimalist architectural underlines or full 1px hairline borders (`#E4DDD3`). Background is pristine ivory (`#FAF8F5`). 
- Focus state: border color transitions to deep espresso (`#1A1816`) with zero glow or drop-shadow. Floating labels use `label-uppercase` in `#706A62`.

### Testimonial & Private Review Carousels
- Full-width espresso block (`#1A1816`) or warm cream panel (`#F5F2EC`). 
- Features oversized italicized quotation marks in serif display, 5-star micro-indicator glyphs in warm sand (`#C5B49E`), and circular client portraits framed by hairline rings.

### Lists & Specifications
- Unordered lists use subtle em-dashes (`—`) or 1px hairline horizontal row separators instead of conventional bullet points. Row items prioritize right-aligned values with generous horizontal spacing.
